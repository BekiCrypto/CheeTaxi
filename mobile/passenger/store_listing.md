### Full Description
**Chebelew (ቼበለው) is Ethiopia’s premier multi-provider ride-hailing and mobility aggregator.**

Compare available rides across Addis Ababa, view transparent upfront pricing, and book your commute with complete peace of mind.

#### Key Features:
- **Multi-Fleet Ride Comparison:** Instantly compare Chebelew's independent driver network alongside licensed fleets (Feres, RIDE, Yango). Find the fastest pickup or the lowest fare in one unified interface.
- **Upfront & Transparent Fares:** Real-time fare calculations conforming strictly to FDRE Ministry of Revenues Regulation No. 570/2025 (15% VAT). Never face unexpected price spikes.
- **Ethiopian Digital Payment Rails:** Pay easily via Telebirr USSD, CBE Birr, commercial bank debit cards, international credit cards (with automatic NBE rate conversion), or cash.
- **Bole Airport Flight Schedules:** Pre-schedule airport pickups and dropoffs with integrated Ethiopian Airlines flight terminal tracking (Terminal 1 & 2).
- **SafeTrip Live Tracking & SOS:** Share an encrypted live tracking link (`chebelew.trustlotto.app/track/...`) with loved ones. Trigger 1-tap emergency assistance with the Ethiopian Federal Police (991) and Chebelew Safety Center (9922).
- **Saved Places:** Bookmark Home, Work, Bole Airport, and custom locations for 1-tap booking.
- **Multilingual Support:** Fully localized in English, Amharic (አማርኛ), Afaan Oromoo, and Tigrinya (ትግርኛ).

## 3. Data Safety & Privacy Disclosures
- **Location:** Approximate & Precise location collected for ride discovery, pickup navigation, and route tracking during active trips.
- **Personal Info:** Name and Phone number for account authentication via Ethio Telecom SMS OTP.
- **Financial Info:** Telebirr and CBE transaction reference IDs for payment receipt reconciliation.
- **Data Encryption:** All data encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS to `https://chebelew.trustlotto.app`.
