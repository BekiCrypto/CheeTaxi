### Full Description
**Earn more on every trip with Chebelew Driver (ቼበለው አሽከርካሪ) — Ethiopia’s 0% platform commission driver app.**

Traditional ride-hailing platforms take 15% to 20% of your hard-earned fares. Chebelew changes the game: independent drivers operate on affordable fixed subscriptions (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly), and **keep 100% of their completed trip fares directly into their own Telebirr or CBE account**.

#### Key Features:
- **0% Platform Commission:** Keep all your earnings. Zero percentage cuts taken from your completed trips.
- **Direct-to-Driver Payments:** Customers pay you directly via your own Telebirr number, CBE Birr account, or In-Car QR code.
- **Daily Net Profitability Intelligence:** Track your daily gross revenue, log fuel and maintenance expenses, and see your true in-pocket take-home earnings with break-even calculations.
- **Corridor Destination Filter ("On-My-Way"):** Heading home to Bole, CMC, Gerji, or Lebu? Activate destination filtering to receive only trip requests along your route.
- **Bole Airport Transit Access:** Receive scheduled airport pickups with confirmed flight details.
- **AI-Assisted KYC Verification:** Fast self-onboarding using Fayda National ID, driver's license, and city taxi permit.
- **Real-time Navigation & Safety HUD:** High-contrast turn-by-turn navigation HUD, 1-click arrival notification, and direct Federal Police (991) emergency dispatch.
- **Multilingual Support:** English, Amharic (አማርኛ), Afaan Oromoo, and Tigrinya (ትግርኛ).

## 3. Background Location Justification for Google Play & Apple App Store
- **Declaration:** "Chebelew Driver collects location data when the app is in use and in the background (while the driver is toggled ONLINE) in order to calculate proximity to nearby ride requests, broadcast real-time vehicle coordinates to matching passengers, and ensure accurate meter fare calculations under FDRE Regulation No. 570/2025. Background location access ceases immediately when the driver toggles OFFLINE or ends their shift."
